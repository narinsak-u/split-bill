# SplitBill

Shared expenses and their resulting balances within groups.

## Language

**Group member**:
A user who belongs to a group and can participate in its expenses.

**Requester**:
The group member acting in a request. A requester may record an expense paid by another group member.

**Expense**:
A cost recorded for a group, paid by one group member and allocated among one or more group members.

**Payer**:
The group member who paid an expense. The payer need not be the requester or a split member.

**Split member**:
A group member assigned a share of an expense; the payer need not participate.

**Share**:
The portion of one expense owed by a split member. Shares together equal the expense amount.

**Balance**:
A group member's total paid minus their total shares in a group. Positive means owed money; negative means owing money.
